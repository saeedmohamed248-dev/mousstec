# برومبتات جيميناي — صور «نماذج أعمال» للمطابع (٦ صور في كل صورة)

كل دفعة: انسخها كلها في محادثة جديدة في جيميناي. الأسماء متخيّلة — لو اسم طلع لشركة معروفة غيّره.
ابعتلي الصور زي ما هي، وأنا هقصّها وأطابقها بالترتيب (الترتيب هو اللي بيحدد المنتج).

## دفعة 01  (p001–p006)

```
You are a professional product photographer building a realistic portfolio for a print shop.
Create ONE single image that is a clean grid of 6 separate photos: 3 columns x 2 rows, landscape overall (about 3:2).

GRID RULES (very important, I will cut the image into 6 pieces):
- 6 equal rectangular panels, each panel landscape 4:3.
- Separate panels with a thick plain WHITE gap (about 3% of the width). No borders, no frames, no shadows between panels.
- Order: left to right, top row first (1 2 3 / 4 5 6).
- NEVER write numbers, codes (like p012), captions or labels on the image or between panels.

PHOTO RULES (every panel):
- Photorealistic e-commerce product photography, soft studio or natural light, sharp focus, realistic setting.
- Each panel shows a printed product made for a DIFFERENT invented business. Put that business's simple original logo
  (flat shape: leaf, cup, star, wave, tooth, crown...) and its name EXACTLY as written below, clean and legible, in English only.
- No other text: no slogans, phone numbers, emails, websites, addresses, QR codes or Arabic text.
- Do NOT use, copy or resemble any real brand, company, logo or trademark (no Apple, VW, Starbucks, etc.); devices and cars have no logos.

Panels:
1. Business cards, 350gsm coated — for "LINNEA CO."
2. Business cards with spot UV — for "IVORIA STUDIO"
3. Embossed business cards — for "ASHCOMBE GROUP"
4. Foil business cards (gold/silver) — for "WRENFORD CO."
5. Clear plastic business cards — for "RIVENHALL STUDIO"
6. Loyalty & discount cards — for "FAYWOOD GROUP"
```
