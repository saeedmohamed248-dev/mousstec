===================== الجزء 1 من 4 — G001 لحد G016 (محادثة جديدة) =====================
You are a professional product photographer. Create photorealistic product photos for an online print-shop marketplace in Egypt (Ramadan and New-Year corporate gifts season). The products must look REAL and PRINTABLE exactly as shown: real paper/card/plastic/fabric/metal materials, real sizes and real finishing (lamination, gold foil, emboss, laser engraving, die-cut, embroidery) that any print shop can produce. Nothing fantasy or impossible.

FORMAT (VERY IMPORTANT):
- Every image is ONE landscape 4:3 picture (2048x1536 or larger) split into a 2x2 GRID of 4 equal panels: top-left, top-right, bottom-left, bottom-right.
- Each panel is a separate, complete product photo of its own item. Thin plain white gap between panels, no borders, no labels, nothing crossing from one panel to another.
- In EVERY reply send 4 such grid images (= 16 product photos), in list order, then stop and wait.
- When I write "كمل" continue with the next 4 grid images. Keep going until the list is finished, then write "DONE" and tell me any grid you skipped.

RULES FOR EVERY PANEL:
- Photorealistic e-commerce product photography, soft natural shadow, sharp focus, high detail, premium feel.
- Each item shows a FICTIONAL company/shop name, so customers imagine their own name on it. Print that name exactly as written, spelled correctly, clean and professional (like a real logo: simple wordmark or monogram). It must look printed/engraved/embossed ON the product, not floating on the photo.
- Arabic text ("رمضان كريم") only where the item asks for it; it must be correct, connected Arabic calligraphy. If you cannot write it correctly, use a decorative crescent instead of text.
- No other text at all: no slogans, phone numbers, emails, websites, dates or random letters. Calendars and planners show blank grids/lines without readable numbers.
- No real brands: props must be plain and unbranded; screens turned off.
- Never write the grid name or the file code on the image.
