## Full description (4000)
Mouss Tec is the official companion app for Mouss Tec subscribers — the management system for auto workshops, spare-parts dealers, print shops and design studios. Sign in with your company code and run your business from your phone.

🎨 For print shops and design studios
• Today at a glance: open and overdue orders, today's deliveries, proofs awaiting the customer and low materials
• New invoice in a minute: customer, items, discount and payment
• Production board: every job in its stage (design → proof → printing → finishing), moved forward with one tap
• Proofs: copy the proof link and send it to your customer on WhatsApp — printing only starts after they approve
• Instant notification when a customer approves a proof or asks for changes
• Customers, quotations, materials and machines

🚗 For workshops and spare-parts dealers
• Work orders: create, follow and move from quotation to delivery
• Inventory and spare parts with camera barcode scanning and low-stock alerts
• Customers, vehicles, maintenance contracts and service reminders
• Treasuries, transactions, suppliers and purchase invoices
• Staff, attendance, leave and salary advances (approve or reject on the go)
• Analytics: weekly revenue and best sellers
• Print or share the work order as PDF

✅ For everyone
• Each employee sees only what their role allows
• Arabic and English
• Your company's currency (EGP, SAR, AED…)
• Encrypted connection, and every company in its own separate database

A Mouss Tec subscription is required (free trial available) — register your company at mousstec.com.
